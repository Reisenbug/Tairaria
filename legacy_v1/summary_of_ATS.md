# Advanced Terraria Strats for LLM

## Biome Order

Jungle is always opposite Dungeon and Snow biome. The underground desert biome is 
almost always on the same side as Jungle. Evil biome tries to avoid Jungle during 
world gen, therefore it is often on the dungeon side. Shimmer is always on the Jungle 
side.

## Main VS Fake jungle entrance

In every world, jungle has a "main cave" (often called just main), going far underground and usually containing many life crystals and chests. While the entrance to main differs between worlds, it generally is a hole in the ground with background walls. 
Recognizing the main cave is a skill that develops as you play, but there are a few things to remember when finding it.
-Jungle also has "hill caves," which are mouths in large hills. Appearing in other biomes as well as the jungle, these are not guaranteed to go far down or have a good amount of loot. 
-Main cave rarely appears on the very edge of the jungle. 
-Main cave almost always opens up to a wider space, however this can be on the surface or below sight.
-You should never have to dig in main cave. The only possible instances are bee hives and jungle temple. However, remember that while main cave can be blocked by beehives [missing image2], the cave continues past that. You can mine through or around the beehive to continue into the cave. The same applies for the lihzard temple, but it is often much harder to go around.
But it is not mean that you cannot mine in jumgle.
So if a cave cuts off or gets too thigh that you would have to dig, you can be sure it's fake entrance (unless it's beehive or temple blocking it)
-When in doubt, it's often better to check a hole in the ground than go past it as it loses much less time if you're wrong.

## Underground desert VS sand dune

There are 2 types of desert: underground desert and sand dunes (we call it fake desert). Only sand dunes have pyramids but ug desert and dunes often overlap.
It is really easy to tell apart if its dune or not. Only ug desert has the visible background walls and it has almost smooth oval shape with sand going really deep into the ground. Dune on the other hand can have shallow sand, so u can see dirt underneath, dunes have hills. Oasis are more common in dunes.

## Go or Not to go down the tree?

Usually, it’s only worth checking the top tree room with the living wood chest and loom. Hoping to find the tunnels is unlikely. 
The exception is when the living tree is connected to the desert. Then going all the way down can find you desert chest with great loot like conch or chisel.

## Feather fall launch(not now)

Holding up with a feather fall (FF for short) produces even more slow-fall. Combined with a hook you can launch yourself often high enough to reach sky islands from the ground. 
How to do it: 
 1. Hold up the whole process (to gain a stronger FF effect)
 2. Hook to a platform or rails (vertically)
 3. Cancel the hook before reaching the platform by jumping
(4. Unequip wings for this, because wings replace vertical momentum with their own)

## Breaking your fall (avoiding fall dmg)
List of items you could use: Cobweb, Honey/Water bucket, Rope with background walls, Mounting on a rail. 
Hook, double jump, umbrella, sky cloud, hitting an enemy,... 

## Rail Slings (“free hook”)
use right-click or mount onto rails but release the hook by jumping. This will give you momentum as if you used a hook.
